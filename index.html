About

About me

I am a Lecturer (tenure-track) at the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Cyprus. I currently hold a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Fellowship, funded by Horizon Europe.

My research lies at the intersection of Bayesian statistics and machine learning, with a focus on probabilistic modelling and inference for complex, structured and evolving data. My interests include graph and network modelling, temporal and spatio-temporal point processes, Bayesian inference, machine learning, generative modelling, and explainability in AI.

I completed my DPhil in Statistics at the University of Oxford, advised by François Caron and Yee Whye Teh. During my doctoral studies, I held visiting researcher positions at the Courant Institute, New York University and the Alan Turing Institute, London, UK. I subsequently held postdoctoral research positions at Imperial College London and at the University of Oxford, where I was a Wolfson College Scholar.

In 2022–2023, I was a Lecturer at the Department of Mathematics and the I-X Centre for AI at Imperial College London.

Currently I serve as an Associate Editor of the Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series C.
